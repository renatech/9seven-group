About 9Seven Group

Practical solutions, delivered by people who show up.

9Seven Group (Pty) Ltd was established in 2017 by Thato Sebongile Nhlapo to deliver practical, technically sound and commercially viable solutions across construction, mechanical engineering, maintenance, facilities, supply and community service environments.

We work as a capable partner for construction maintenance, mechanical engineering support, trade-based artisan services, motor mechanic services, general supply, catering and facilities support — grounded in safety, integrity, hands-on management and reliable client communication.

We use our platform to support socio-economic development, black empowerment, employment creation and skills transfer, while delivering measurable value to public and private sector clients.

Mission

To provide cost-effective services without compromising quality, safety or professional client relationships.

Vision

To be recognised as a leading black economic empowerment company contributing to infrastructure, services and technical skills development.

Values

Integrity, accountability, professionalism, safety, teamwork, communication and service excellence.

Development Focus

Sustainable employment, skills transfer and participation of previously disadvantaged communities in the economy.

Quality, Safety & Delivery

No work runs without a competent person supervising it.

That single rule protects the client, the workforce and the quality of the final output — it's the routing every job follows, start to finish.

01

Understand Scope

Confirm client need, site conditions, outputs, budget and timeframe.

02

Plan & Resource

Allocate supervisors, artisans, technicians, equipment, materials and safety controls.

03

Execute Safely

Deliver the work using competent teams, PPE, safe work procedures and method statements where required.

04

Quality Check

Inspect workmanship, correct snags and document completion.

05

Handover & Support

Submit completion notes, service records and client sign-off.
